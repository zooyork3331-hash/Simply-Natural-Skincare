GOOD TO KNOW

A few little questions.

Curious about our products or placing an inquiry? We're happy to help.

Ask us anything ↗
How do I inquire about a product?+

Browse the products, add your favorites to your inquiry bag, and copy or email your request. Mention your preferred scent, if any. We’ll confirm the availability, ingredients, sizing, and pricing before a purchase is made.

What ingredients are in the products?+

Ingredients may differ by product. Please request the full ingredient list for the item you are interested in, especially if you have allergies or sensitive skin.

Do you offer local pickup or shipping?+

Pickup, delivery, and shipping options are confirmed directly when you inquire. Nothing is charged through this website.

Can body scrubs be used on the face?+

Use each product only as directed. Body scrubs aren't necessarily formulated for delicate facial skin. Avoid applying to broken or irritated skin, and discontinue use if irritation occurs.

Are the products shown on this site available now?+

The product types and scents shown come from our current menu, but specific scent pairings, stock, prices, and sizes can change. Please check availability before ordering.

Can I request a specific scent or a custom order?+

You're welcome to ask about your preference. Custom requests are not guaranteed and will need to be confirmed directly.

How do I care for sensitive skin?+

Ask for the full ingredients and usage directions for the specific product before trying it. Consider patch-testing as directed and avoid scrubbing irritated or broken skin.
